Before you write

Little questions, honest answers

What do you need from me to get started?

Your website address, what you want it to achieve, and view access to Google Search Console and Analytics if you have them. Don't worry if you don't, we can sort that out together.

Can you guarantee rankings?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Search engines decide, not me. What I can promise is careful work, clear reporting and steady improvements.

How long does SEO take?

Usually months rather than weeks. Quick technical fixes can show up earlier, while content and authority take longer to build.

Do you work with local businesses?

Yes. Local SEO is one of the areas I work in, alongside technical, on-page and off-page SEO.
